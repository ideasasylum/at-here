# Design System: at-here

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Neon Bar"**

The contact card is a cue list read down one dark wall of a neon bar. Identity is the sign over the door: the handle set in Tilt Neon and lit magenta. Below it, one lit shelf per app the account has written to. Every shelf is separated from the last by a 2px neon tube whose colour walks cyan to violet to magenta to orange in rank order; the utilities sit under an amber tube and the record footer under a warm white one. Surfaces stay near-black with a faint tint of the tube above them, and light spills from each tube 180px into the shelf below. Colour encodes reading order and nothing else.

Nothing large is solid and saturated. The tubes, the glow around display type, and one magenta button are the only bright fills on the page; everything else is dark surface and light ink. Regions meet at a lit line, never at a hard edge between two flat colours, and nothing casts a shadow: every box-shadow and text-shadow in the world is a zero-offset glow, light emitted rather than light blocked. Type does two jobs: Tilt Neon glows for the sign, the app names and section heads; Saira at 17px with tabular numerals carries everything else in a plain factual register.

The system refuses the link-in-bio scaffold: no avatar circle, no stacked pill buttons, no per-app brand colours or logos, no theme toggle. The avatar is a rectangular photograph in a neon-rimmed frame standing on the first tube at the foot of the first viewport.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark tinted surfaces; only the tubes and their glow are bright
- Tube colour derived from rank position along a fixed cyan-violet-magenta-orange ramp
- Surface, display ink and secondary ink all derived from the tube by fixed mixes
- Tilt Neon glowing in tube colour for identity and heads; Saira for everything else
- Every shadow is a zero-offset glow; nothing casts
- Motion is a stage raise in cue order plus a one-time flicker as the sign ignites; all removed under reduced motion

## Colors

A near-black room with a violet cast, six neon tubes, and a small family of light inks; every per-band colour is mixed from its tube.

### Primary
- **Cyan** (`{colors.cyan}`): the first tube, the wordmark's glow, the stage horizon on the home and error pages, the caret, the scrollbar thumb, the secondary button stroke, the lookup input stroke and focus ring, the step counters, and the global focus outline. Cyan is the browser's colour.
- **Violet** (`{colors.violet}`): the second stop of the tube ramp.
- **Magenta** (`{colors.magenta}`): the third stop of the tube ramp, the handle's glow on every card, text selection, and the one primary action fill.
- **Orange** (`{colors.orange}`): the last stop of the tube ramp.

### Secondary
- **Amber** (`{colors.amber}`): the utility band's tube (Save this card) and the readout's unverified-handle warning.
- **Warm** (`{colors.warm}`): the record footer's tube; warm white rather than a hue so the footer reads as house light.

- **Stage Crown** (`{colors.stage-crown}`): the inner stop of the radial gradient behind the first viewport (`120% 80% at 50% -10%`, to bg at 60%), a faint violet cast over the top of the page.

### Named Rules
**The Readable Tube Rule.** Small text set in a tube colour (rank numerals, the Unlisted tag) uses `--tube-text`: the tube mixed toward white in 5% steps until it clears 4.6:1 against its surface. Cyan, magenta, orange, amber and warm need no lightening; the violet stop does.

**The Rank Ramp Rule.** A cue's tube is `scale(TUBES, i / (n - 1))` where `TUBES = [cyan, violet, magenta, orange]`, `i` is the app's zero-based rank and `n` the app count; linear RGB interpolation between adjacent stops. One app is cyan; two apps are cyan and orange; four apps land exactly on the four stops. Utilities are always amber, the record footer always warm. Colour never signals importance, only order.

**The Six Percent Rule.** A band's surface is its base mixed 6% toward its tube (`mix(SURFACE, tube, 0.06)`; the record footer mixes from the darker record base). That is the whole tint: surfaces never exceed 6% of the tube, and the tube itself never fills an area larger than its 2px line.

**The Tube Ink Rule.** Display text lit by a tube is set in the tube mixed 72% toward white (`mix(tube, #FFFFFF, 0.72)`), then glowed with the tube colour. The pale body keeps the glyphs legible (12.9:1 or better on every surface); the tube lives in the shadow layers.

**The Secondary Ink Rule.** Secondary ink starts at the tube mixed 45% toward ink and walks toward ink in 10% steps until it reaches 4.6:1 against the surface. On these dark surfaces the first candidate always clears (8:1 or better), so in practice `--ink-2` is `mix(tube, INK, 0.45)`: a tinted ink that carries the tube's hue into the notes and collection names.

**The Bright Things Rule.** The only saturated fills on a page are the 2px tubes, the magenta primary button, the selection highlight, and glows. Never fill a band, card, header or footer with a tube colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Tilt Neon (with Trebuchet MS, sans-serif), weight 400 only, mixed case
**Body Font:** Saira (with Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif), weights 400, 500, 600
**Label/Mono Font:** `code` in ui-monospace at 0.92em; labels are Saira 500 tracked uppercase; numerals are Saira with `tabular-nums` set on body

**Character:** Tilt Neon is a soft rounded display face whose letters already read as bent glass tube; lit with a layered glow it becomes signage. Saira is a squared geometric sans that stays plain and factual under it. Display type is rare: identity (handle, wordmark, app names) and section heads glow; nothing else does.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, `clamp(2.6rem, 7.4vw, 5.6rem)`, 1.02, 0.005em, max 16ch): the handle on the card stage in magenta glass with the magenta glow, `padding-bottom: .04em` so the glow is not clipped. Dots in handles get a `<wbr>` after them. Variants: DID as handle `clamp(1.5rem, 4.2vw, 3.2rem)` with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; home h1 `clamp(3rem, 9.5vw, 5.6rem)` balanced; error h1 `clamp(2rem, 5.8vw, 4rem)` max 22ch, balanced.
- **Headline** (400, `clamp(1.7rem, 3vw, 2.4rem)`, 1, 0.01em): the app name on each cue, in tube ink with the tube glow. Fixed 1.6rem below 480px. The example cue on home and error uses `clamp(1.5rem, 2.4vw, 2.2rem)`.
- **Title** (400, `clamp(1.5rem, 2.4vw, 2rem)`, 1, 0.01em): the utility band's Save this card, amber tube ink and glow.
- **Section** (400, 1.5rem, 1, 0.01em, 18px below): record footer h2s in warm tube ink with a softer two-layer glow.
- **Wordmark** (400, 1.2rem, 1, 0.02em): at-here top left, cyan glass with the cyan glow; white on hover.
- **Name** (600, `clamp(1.35rem, 2.2vw, 1.7rem)`, 1.2, -0.01em): display name beneath the handle, followed by the headline line at 1.08rem in ink body.
- **Lede** (400, `clamp(1.1rem, 1.6vw, 1.3rem)`, 1.55): home and error intro, ink body with 600 ink emphasis.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.55): everything unlabelled, capped at 62ch (utility prose 52ch). Blurbs 1rem in ink body; notes 0.95rem in secondary ink clamped to two lines; step list 0.98rem in ink dim; readout values 0.92rem; record list, terms, credit, warning and tertiary link share the 0.875rem step.
- **Action** (600, 0.95rem, 1, 0.02em): the Open/Visit label, both buttons, the lookup submit. The input is 500 at 1.05rem.
- **Label** (500, 0.7rem, 1.6, 0.14em, uppercase): readout terms. Record terms 0.68rem/1.7. Evidence lines 500 0.72rem/1.7 at 0.1em with 600 ink counts. Nav link 500 0.875rem at 0.12em; Full bio cue 500 0.8rem at 0.12em; rank 500 0.82rem at 0.1em in the readable tube tint (`--tube-text`), zero-padded, not uppercase; step counter 600 0.78rem.
- **Tag** (600, 0.66rem, 1, 0.14em, uppercase): the Unlisted chip, stroked and coloured in raw tube colour with no glow.

### Named Rules
**The Lit Type Rule.** Tilt Neon appears only lit: handle, wordmark, app names, section heads, each carrying its tube's glow. It is never set unglowed and never used for body, labels, buttons or numerals.

**The Neon Glow Recipe.** A lit heading's `text-shadow` is four layers from a hot core outward: `0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,.5)`, `0 0 6px tube`, `0 0 18px rgba(tube,.6)`, `0 0 40px rgba(tube,.3)`. The handle scales it up (`1px white .7, 8px, 24px, 64px at .55`); the wordmark drops the white core (`6px, 16px at .7, 34px at .3`); record heads use two layers (`6px at .7, 18px at .35`). Hover on a cue brightens the app name to `1px .8, 8px, 26px .85, 60px .45` over 180ms.

**The Label-Is-Data Rule.** Tracked uppercase micro-type appears only as a `<dt>` naming a value, an evidence line naming record collections, a rank numeral, or a status tag. It is never placed above a heading as a kicker or eyebrow.

**The Tabular Data Rule.** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` is set on body and inherited; counts (Apps, Collections, collection totals) are 600 weight so they read as data.

## Layout

One centred column, `max-width: 1240px`, fluid gutter `clamp(20px, 5vw, 72px)`. Every band is full-bleed in surface and tube; content sits in the shared `.wrap` column so the tubes run edge to edge while text stays on the grid.

**Stage (first viewport).** Two-column grid `minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(220px, 300px)`, gap `40px clamp(32px, 5vw, 80px)`, padding top `clamp(40px, 7vh, 84px)`, bottom `clamp(120px, 22vh, 220px)`, `min-height: calc(72svh - 60px)`. Handle, name, headline line and bio fill the left; the readout `<dl>` (auto / 1fr, gap `10px 18px`, 14%-white hairline above, `padding-top: clamp(12px, 2vh, 26px)`) sits top right. The horizon spill fills the bottom `min(34vh, 280px)`; the actor stands bottom-right on the column edge at `clamp(96px, 12vw, 168px)` wide, 3:4, shifted 1px down onto the first tube. Home and error stages are one column with no minimum height, bottom padding `clamp(64px, 9vh, 104px)`, and the lookup form `clamp(28px, 5vh, 44px)` below the lede.

**Cue band.** Grid `minmax(0, 7fr) minmax(0, 8fr) auto`, gap `10px clamp(24px, 4vw, 56px)`, items centred, padding 46px top and 40px bottom, `min-height: 150px`. Column one is the lead (rank and app name, baseline aligned, gap 18px); two is the about stack (blurb, note, evidence at 6px); three is the action with 14px vertical padding.

**Utility band.** Grid `minmax(0, 1fr) auto`, gap `20px 48px`, padding 64px top, 60px bottom. Actions wrap in a 12px flex row.

**Record band.** Grid `minmax(0, 3fr) minmax(0, 2fr)`, gap `40px clamp(32px, 5vw, 80px)`, padding 72px top, 56px bottom, `flex: 1` to fill to the viewport floor. Collection list in two CSS columns (32px gap, 2px row padding, 7%-white hairlines); credit spans both columns under a 10%-white hairline with 26px above.

**Tube rhythm.** Every band's top padding clears the 2px tube and the brightest part of its 180px spill before text begins; the tube itself is never overlapped by content.

**860px and below.** Stage collapses to one column, 30px gap, 64px bottom padding; readout narrows to `calc(100% - 150px)` with `min-height: 120px` to leave room for the actor, which shrinks to `clamp(88px, 26vw, 120px)` at the gutter. Cue grid becomes `minmax(0, 1fr) auto` (padding 34px/28px, no min height): lead row one, about stack spanning row two, action reduced to a 28px arrow with its label visually hidden. Utility (48px/44px) and record (56px top) go single column; collections one column; lookup stacks with a full-width `16px 22px` submit.

**480px and below.** Readout becomes one column (`gap: 2px 0`, 10px above each term, narrowed to `calc(100% - 130px)`); app names fix at 1.6rem; the Unlisted tag drops to its own line as a block.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is emitted, not cast. There are no offset shadows anywhere: every `box-shadow` and `text-shadow` is centred (`0 0 blur`) so it reads as light leaving a source. Surfaces are flat and dark; depth comes from the tubes, the spill of tube light down into the surface below, the glow around lit type, and the tinted surface itself. Nothing is layered, nothing floats, no container has a border except the actor's rim.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Tube glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 6px rgb(tube), 0 0 18px rgba(tube,.7), 0 0 48px rgba(tube,.45)` on the 2px line): the seam of every band.
- **Tube spill** (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(tube,.16) 0%, rgba(tube,.04) 55%, rgba(tube,0) 100%)`, 180px tall, below the tube): light falling onto the shelf.
- **Horizon spill** (`linear-gradient(0deg, rgba(tube,.20) 0%, rgba(tube,.06) 45%, rgba(tube,0) 100%)`, `min(34vh, 280px)` tall at the stage foot): the first tube lighting the stage from below; hidden in print.
- **Actor rim** (`0 0 0 1px rgba(tube,.15), 0 0 28px rgba(tube,.35)` with a 1px 70% tube border): the avatar's frame lit by the first tube.
- **Neon text glow**: see the Neon Glow Recipe under Typography.
- **Primary action glow** (`0 0 14px rgba(magenta,.55), 0 0 40px rgba(magenta,.25)`; hover `0 0 18px .75, 0 0 56px .35`): the magenta button.
- **Secondary action glow** (`0 0 12px rgba(cyan,.3), inset 0 0 14px rgba(cyan,.08)`; hover `0 0 18px .5, inset 14px .12`): the cyan-stroked button, lit from within.
- **Input focus** (`0 0 0 3px rgba(cyan,.18), 0 0 18px rgba(cyan,.45)` with a cyan border): the lookup field.
- **Step counter glow** (`0 0 10px rgba(cyan,.35)`): the home page's numbered circles.
- **Focus glow** (`0 0 12px rgba(cyan,.6)` with a 2px cyan outline at 4px offset): the global `:focus-visible`.

### Named Rules
**The Zero Offset Rule.** Every shadow is `0 0 <blur>`; nothing has an x or y offset and nothing is black. A shadow with an offset is a cast shadow and does not belong in this world.

**The Raise Rule.** Bands enter with `@keyframes raise` (`filter: brightness(.45)` to 1) over 0.8s on `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`, delayed `min(i * 80ms, 640ms) + 120ms` by band index (`--i`) so the wall lights top to bottom. The handle ignites once: `@keyframes ignite`, 1.4s, `steps(1, end)`, opacity `.25, .9, .35, 1, hold, .6, 1` at 0/6/9/12/30/33/35%. Hover transitions are 160–180ms. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every animation and transition is removed.

## Shapes

Near-square glass and tube. Interactive controls carry a 4px radius (buttons, lookup input and submit); the Unlisted tag and focus rings 2px; the actor rounds only its top corners at 4px because its foot stands on the tube. The single circle is the 26px numbered step counter on the home page. Tubes are straight 2px lines running edge to edge; an unlisted app's tube is dashed, `repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, tube 0 18px, transparent 18px 26px)`, an 18px dash with an 8px gap. Borders are hairlines: 1px at 7–14% white for rules (readout, list rows, credit), 1px tube colour for the tag and actor rim, 1.5px for control strokes (buttons, input, step counter). No diagonals, no clipping, no skews.

## Components

### Cue band
The signature component: one full-width lit shelf per app, the whole band a link.
- **Anatomy:** tube (2px `::before` in raw tube colour with the tube glow), spill (180px `::after` gradient), hold overlay (`.hold`, inset 0, `rgba(tube,.07)`), rank (`01`, readable tube tint `--tube-text`, `min-width: 2ch`), app name (Headline in tube ink with the neon glow, optional Unlisted tag), about stack (blurb in ink body, optional two-line note in secondary ink, evidence line in secondary ink with 600 ink counts), action (`Open {app}` or `Visit {app}` in Action type with a 24px stroked arrow in raw tube colour).
- **Colour:** set per instance as inline custom properties `--surface`, `--tube`, `--tube-rgb`, `--tube-ink`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--i` from `themeFor(tubeFor(i, n))`. The stylesheet knows no band colour.
- **Hover / Focus:** hold overlay fades to `rgba(tube,.07)` over 180ms; app name glow brightens; arrow slides 6px right on the ease curve. Active swaps the hold to `rgba(0,0,0,.22)`. Focus is a 2px outline in raw tube colour inset 10px, with the global focus glow suppressed.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** derive every band's colour from rank with `tubeFor(i, n)` and every surface and ink with `themeFor`; pass them as inline `--surface`, `--tube`, `--tube-rgb`, `--tube-ink`, `--tube-text`, `--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--i`.
- **Do** keep surfaces dark: a band is its base mixed 6% toward its tube, and the tube is bright only in its 2px line, its glow and its 180px spill.
- **Do** set Tilt Neon only where it glows (handle, wordmark, app names, section heads) in tube ink with the four-layer neon recipe; set everything else in Saira with tabular numerals.
- **Do** make every shadow a zero-offset glow in a tube colour; the hot core, if any, is white at low alpha.
- **Do** mark hover and focus on a cue with the 7% tube hold overlay and a brighter name glow, and focus with a 2px tube-colour outline inset 10px.
- **Do** raise bands in cue order with `--i` delays capped at 640ms, ignite the handle once, and remove all animation and transition under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** mark unrecognised apps with the dashed tube and the Unlisted tag, never by omitting them.

### Don't:
- **Don't** fill a band, header, footer or card with a saturated tube colour; the only saturated fills are the tubes, the magenta primary button, selection and glows.
- **Don't** let two regions meet at a hard edge between flat colours; every seam is a lit tube with spill.
- **Don't** cast a shadow: no offset box-shadow, no black shadow, no long shadow under the actor.
- **Don't** build the link-in-bio scaffold: no avatar circle, no stacked pill buttons, no centred column of equal chips.
- **Don't** give an app its brand colour or logo; tube colour means order, and glyphs are limited to the arrow, search and warning strokes.
- **Don't** place tracked uppercase text above a heading as a kicker or eyebrow; micro-type names a value, a collection, a rank or a status.
- **Don't** hand-assign a surface or ink on a band; every per-band colour is a fixed mix from its tube.
- **Don't** add a theme toggle or a light mode; the room is dark.
- **Don't** use radii above 4px on anything but the step counter circle.
